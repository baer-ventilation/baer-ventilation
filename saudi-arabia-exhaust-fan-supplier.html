Exhaust Fan Supplier for Saudi Arabia

60Hz-rated ventilation fans engineered for Saudi heat — supplied factory-direct to Jeddah, Dammam, Riyadh and NEOM-region projects.

Saudi Arabia runs on a 220V/60Hz grid, which immediately rules out most European 50Hz stock. Running a 50Hz motor on 60Hz raises speed and current draw, shortens motor life and voids warranty — a problem we see repeatedly when importers buy generic 50Hz fans and ship them to the Kingdom. Every BAER model for Saudi Arabia is supplied with a genuine 60Hz motor, and our testing lab verifies performance at 60Hz before shipment.

Market Overview

Saudi Arabia is the largest ventilation market in the Gulf, driven by giga-projects (NEOM, The Red Sea, Qiddiya), a national housing programme, and hotel and retail construction under Vision 2030. Demand concentrates on residential bathroom and kitchen extraction, plus commercial kitchen and parking ventilation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply genuine 60Hz motors for Saudi Arabia?

Yes. All models destined for Saudi Arabia are built with 220V/60Hz motors and performance-tested at 60Hz before packing. This is standard, not an upcharge.

Can you support SABER product registration?

We provide the technical file, CB/IEC test reports, model photos and factory information your Saudi importer or certification partner needs to register products in SABER and obtain shipment CoCs.

MOQ is 100 pcs per model (mixed models in one container accepted). Lead time is typically 15–25 days after deposit, plus 18–25 days sea transit to Jeddah or Dammam.
