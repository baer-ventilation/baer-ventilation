Exhaust Fan Supplier for Saudi Arabia

60Hz-rated ventilation fans engineered for Saudi heat — supplied factory-direct to Jeddah, Dammam, Riyadh and NEOM-region projects.

Saudi Arabia runs on a 220V/60Hz grid, which immediately rules out most European 50Hz stock. Running a 50Hz motor on 60Hz raises speed and current draw, shortens motor life and voids warranty — a problem we see repeatedly when importers buy generic 50Hz fans and ship them to the Kingdom. Every BAER model for Saudi Arabia is supplied with a genuine 60Hz motor, and our testing lab verifies performance at 60Hz before shipment.

Voltage and Frequency: Why 60Hz Matters

Saudi Arabia runs on 230V / 60Hz — a combination that differs from most of the Gulf, which uses 50Hz. A fan built for 50Hz will spin roughly 20% faster on 60Hz, drawing more current and shortening motor life. That is why we supply genuine 60Hz motors for Saudi orders rather than relabelling 50Hz stock. Always confirm voltage and frequency in your inquiry.

Climate Considerations: Heat and Dust

Saudi Arabia's climate punishes fans in two specific ways: ambient heat and fine dust.

  • Heat: motors must be rated for continuous high-ambient operation. We specify motors with adequate temperature class for the Gulf, rather than domestic-market motors that overheat on a 45°C rooftop.
  • Dust: sand and fine particulate shorten bearing life. For outdoor and industrial sites we recommend sealed ball-bearing motors and, where the application allows, shutters that close when the fan is off.

These are not optional upgrades in the Kingdom — they are the difference between a fan that lasts one season and one that lasts its full warranty. Tell us the installation environment (indoor / outdoor / dusty) in your inquiry and we will specify accordingly.
