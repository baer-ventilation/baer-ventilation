Ordering & MOQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? ＋
Standard stock models: MOQ 100 pcs. OEM/custom models: MOQ 500 pcs. For first orders and samples, we offer flexible quantities — contact us to discuss.
Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order? ＋
Yes. We ship single-unit samples for evaluation, freight collect (Samples available — freight collect). The sample fee is usually credited back against your first bulk order.
What information do you need to quote me accurately? ＋
Application (bathroom / kitchen / warehouse / ducted), required airflow (CFM or m³/h), voltage and frequency, target quantity, and destination port. With these five details we can quote within 24 hours.
Do you allow mixed models in one container? ＋
Yes. You can combine multiple fan types and sizes in a single 20GP or 40HQ container, as long as each model meets its minimum quantity (typically 100 pcs per model).

We accept T/T (bank transfer), L/C at sight, and Western Union. Standard terms: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment. For long-term partners, we offer flexible arrangements.

Certifications

Do you provide CE and RoHS certificates? ＋
Yes. All standard models carry CE and RoHS certification. Test reports and Declaration of Conformity are provided with every shipment. SAA certification is available for Australia-market models on request.
Which markets do your products meet the standards for? ＋
Our products are certified for multiple markets: CE (Europe), CCC (China), SAA (Australia), CB (International), and ETL (North America). We can advise on the correct certification for your target market.
Do I need SABER certification to import fans into Saudi Arabia? ＋
Yes. Saudi Arabia requires SABER / SASO product certification for electrical fans. We regularly ship to Saudi Arabia and can provide the required technical files and support your SABER registration with the certificate of conformity.
What certifications do I need for the EU market? ＋
For the EU you need CE marking backed by a Declaration of Conformity and LVD/EMC test reports. If the motor uses an external power supply, RoHS also applies. Our standard models are already CE certified.
Can you provide CB test reports for local certification? ＋
Yes. We can provide CB scheme test reports to help you complete local certifications in many countries without retesting from scratch.

Products & Sizing

How do I calculate what CFM exhaust fan I need? ＋
Use our free CFM calculator. The formula is CFM = (Length × Width × Height) × air changes per hour ÷ 60. A bathroom typically needs 8 air changes per hour, a kitchen 15.
What is the difference between ceiling, wall and window exhaust fans? ＋
Ceiling fans mount in the ceiling and duct to the outside; wall fans mount through a wall opening; window fans fit in a window frame. Ceiling is ideal for bathrooms, wall for kitchens and workshops, window for rooms without duct space.
Which is quieter — an inline duct fan or an axial fan? ＋
An inline (mixed-flow) duct fan is generally quieter because the duct absorbs sound and the impeller is designed for lower turbulence. Axial fans move more air at low pressure but are noisier. See our inline duct fan vs axial fan guide.
Do you make silent bathroom fans? ＋
Yes, we produce a dedicated silent series for bathrooms and bedrooms. We publish the measured noise rating (dB) for each model and can send test reports on request.
Can you customize voltage for different countries? ＋
Yes. We support 110V/60Hz (North America), 220–240V/50Hz (Europe, Middle East, Australia), and custom voltage requirements. Just specify in your inquiry.

How are the fans packed to prevent damage? ＋
Each fan is packed in an individual carton with foam or EPS protection, then loaded on export pallets or into reinforced cartons for container shipping. We send pre-shipment packing photos before every shipment.

Standard in-stock models: 3–7 days. Standard production orders: 25–35 days. OEM/custom mold orders: 45–60 days (including tooling). We provide weekly production updates and pre-shipment photos.
