Frequently Asked Questions

Everything B2B buyers need to know before placing an order with BAER Ventilation.

Home › FAQ
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? ＋
Standard stock models: MOQ 100 pcs. OEM/custom models: MOQ 500 pcs. For first orders and samples, we offer flexible quantities — contact us to discuss.
Do you provide CE and RoHS certificates? ＋
Yes. All standard models carry CE and RoHS certification. Test reports and Declaration of Conformity are provided with every shipment. SAA certification is available for Australia market models on request.
What is the lead time for mass production? ＋
  • Standard in-stock models: 3–7 days
  • Standard production orders: 25–35 days
  • OEM/custom mold orders: 45–60 days (including tooling)

We provide weekly production updates and pre-shipment photos.

Can you customize voltage for different countries? ＋
Yes. We support 110V/60Hz (North America), 220–240V/50Hz (Europe, Middle East, Australia), and custom voltage requirements. Just specify in your inquiry.
What warranty do you offer? ＋
2-year warranty on all products. Defective units within warranty period will be replaced or refunded after video/photo confirmation. We also offer lifetime technical support and spare parts supply.
Do you offer OEM and private label services? ＋
Yes. We provide full OEM services including custom branding, packaging, voltage specifications, and mold development. Minimum OEM order is 500 pcs. Contact us with your requirements and we will provide a tailored quote within 24 hours.
What payment terms do you accept? ＋
We accept T/T (bank transfer), L/C at sight, and Western Union. Standard terms: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment. For long-term partners, we offer flexible payment arrangements.
Which markets do your products meet the standards for? ＋
Our products are certified for multiple markets: CE (Europe), CCC (China), SAA (Australia), CB (International), and ETL (North America). We can advise on the correct certification for your target market.

Still have questions?

Our export team responds within 2 hours during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9:00–18:00 CST).
