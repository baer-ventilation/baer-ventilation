BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between standard and double-grill models? +
Standard models have a single grille and louvre. Double-grill models add a second grille for better airflow direction control, ideal for through-wall installations.
Can this fan be installed on glass windows? +
Yes — optional side panels allow installation in glass windows and wooden frames. Self-tapping screws are included.
Is it suitable for outdoor use? +
The ABS housing is UV and weather resistant, so it can be installed on exterior walls and windows. For direct rain exposure we recommend adding a rain hood.
What is your MOQ? +
Stock models: MOQ 100 pcs. OEM with custom logo: MOQ 500 pcs.
