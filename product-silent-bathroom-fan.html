BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this fan safe to install above a shower? +
The IP44 rating makes it suitable for bathroom zone 2 (outside the immediate shower area). For installation directly above a shower we can advise on suitable models.
Can I get humidity-sensor versions? +
Yes — automatic models with a built-in humidity sensor turn on when moisture rises and run a timed after-run to clear condensation.
Do you supply for hotel projects? +
Yes. We regularly supply bathroom fans for hotel and apartment projects in the Middle East. Bulk pricing and fast production are available.
What certificates do you provide? +
CE for standard models. Test reports and Declaration of Conformity accompany every shipment.
