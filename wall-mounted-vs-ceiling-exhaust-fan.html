Wall Mounted vs Ceiling Exhaust Fan: Which to Choose

Last updated: September 3, 2026

A practical comparison to help you pick the right mounting type.

Wall-mounted and ceiling-mounted exhaust fans both extract air, but they suit different rooms and different construction. This guide lays out the trade-offs.

The Difference at a Glance

FactorWall-mounted fanCeiling-mounted fan
Where it ventsDirectly through an outside wallThrough the ceiling and a duct to the outside
Best forKitchens, workshops, rooms on an outside wallBathrooms, internal rooms without an outside wall
InstallationSimple — one hole in the wallMore involved — duct run required
NoiseExposed, usually louderCan be quieter (especially inline)
CostLowerHigher (fan + duct + labour)

When to Choose a Wall Fan

  • The room has a direct outside wall and no ducting is possible.
  • You need high airflow at low cost (kitchens, workshops, warehouses).

When to Choose a Ceiling Fan

  • The room is internal, or you want a flush, concealed finish.
  • You need quiet operation in a bathroom or bedroom.

For ceiling installs with any duct run, choose an inline mixed-flow fan so the airflow survives the duct. Browse our ceiling-mount and wall/window ranges for models.
