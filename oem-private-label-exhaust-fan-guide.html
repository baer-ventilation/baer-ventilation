OEM & Private Label Exhaust Fans: The Complete Guide

Last updated: September 3, 2026

How to turn a standard exhaust fan into your own branded product line.

OEM — original equipment manufacturing — means the factory builds the product to your specification and puts your brand on it. For importers and distributors, OEM is how a generic exhaust fan becomes your product line. This guide covers what you can customise, how the process works, and what it costs.

What You Can Customise

ElementWhat is possible
BrandingYour logo on the grille, housing and nameplate
PackagingCustom cartons, manuals and inserts in your language
Voltage & frequency110V/60Hz, 220-240V/50Hz, or country-specific
Colour & finishHousing colour and finish to match your range
ConfigurationMotor type, cable length, plug, accessories
New mouldA completely new housing or grille design (higher MOQ)

The OEM Process, Step by Step

  • 1. Specification: you confirm the model, voltage, branding and packaging requirements.
  • 2. Sampling: we produce a pre-production sample for your approval (this is where most adjustments happen).
  • 3. Production: after sample approval, mass production runs 25–35 days, or 45–60 days with a new mould.
  • 4. Inspection & shipping: pre-shipment photos, optional third-party inspection, then export packing.

Mould Development: What to Expect

A new mould — for a custom housing or grille — is the single biggest OEM cost and the longest lead-time item. The mould cost is typically amortised across your order or charged separately, and the mould remains available for your repeat orders. For most importers, starting with an existing housing and adding your branding is the fastest, lowest-risk path to a private-label line.

OEM MOQ and Lead Time

  • MOQ: 500 pcs per model for OEM with branding; higher for a new mould.
  • Lead time: 25–35 days for branding on an existing model, 45–60 days with tooling.
