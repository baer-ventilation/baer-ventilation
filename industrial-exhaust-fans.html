Industrial exhaust fans do one job: move hot air, fumes and dust out of a building fast enough that people can work and goods do not spoil. Choosing one is not about brand — it is about matching airflow and mounting to the space. This page explains which of our four fan families fits which industrial space, how to calculate the airflow you need, and what to send us to get an exact quote in 24 hours.

Which Fan Fits Which Job

Production floor / workshop

Removes welding fume, heat and airborne dust. Wall shutter fans 8″–12″ mounted high on the external wall, opposite the fresh-air inlet.

Warehouse & storage hall

Stops condensation on stored goods and clears stale air. KLT panel exhaust fans 20B–30B, or large shutter fans for high bays.

Loading dock / dispatch

Clears vehicle exhaust during loading. Wall shutter fans 12″ or KLT-30B, interlocked with the dock door where possible.

Packing & assembly line

Operator comfort rather than process extraction. 6″–8″ shutter fans or KLT-10B–18B at regular intervals.

Control room / workshop office

Quiet, low-vibration air change next to the plant floor. Silent cabinet fans ducted to the outside.

Ducted run to an outside wall

When the fan cannot sit on the wall itself. BXF mixed-flow duct fans, 100–315 mm, for runs up to several metres.

SIZING

How Much Airflow You Actually Need

Industrial ventilation is sized by air changes per hour (ACH): the building volume multiplied by how many times the air should be replaced each hour. Typical design values by space:

Space typeTypical air changes per hourNotes
General warehouse / storage6 – 8 ACHRaise it if goods are moisture-sensitive
Assembly / packing workshop8 – 12 ACHOccupant comfort driven
Welding / fabrication bay12 – 15 ACHLocal extraction still recommended at the arc
Loading dock / vehicle area10 – 15 ACHHigher during peak dispatch hours
Storage with condensation risk4 – 6 ACHCombine with dehumidification in humid climates

Worked example. A hall of 20 m × 10 m × 5 m = 1,000 m³ used as a workshop at 10 ACH needs 10,000 m³/h, which is about 5,900 CFM. That is six 6″ units at 984 CFM each, or two larger 12″ units — the choice depends on how many wall openings you have.

These are design starting points, not a substitute for your HVAC engineer’s calculation. Send us the building dimensions and we will size it with you — or use the CFM calculator and read the longer airflow calculation guide.

BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What size exhaust fan do I need for a 1,000 m³ warehouse? +
At 6–8 air changes per hour a 1,000 m³ store needs 6,000–8,000 m³/h, roughly 3,500–4,700 CFM. That is four to five 6″ wall shutter fans, or two 12″ units. Send the room dimensions and we will confirm the count.
Can you supply 60Hz or 110V versions? +
Yes. 220-240V/50Hz is standard. 110V/60Hz for the US and Canada, and 60Hz versions for Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Iraq, are produced to order. State the voltage on the enquiry and it is built into the quote.
Does the shutter stop air coming back in? +
The automatic back-draft louvre closes when the fan stops, which stops outside air, rain and dust entering through the opening. It is not a sealed damper — for a fully airtight line you need a separate motorised damper.
Is the motor rated for continuous running? +
Yes. These are continuous-duty motors, and every unit is test-run on the line before packing. For dust-heavy or high-temperature environments tell us the condition so the winding class and insulation can be matched.
Can the fan be mounted with ducting instead of on the wall? +
A wall shutter fan is designed for direct wall or window mounting. If the fan has to sit away from the wall, use the BXF mixed-flow duct fan instead — it is built for duct runs of several metres and comes in 100–315 mm sizes.
What certifications come with the fan? +
CE is standard. The factory holds ISO 9001, CCC and CB, with certificate numbers published on this page. ETL can be arranged on request for US distribution. SAA for Australia is renewed per order — it is not currently held.
Do you supply spare parts and motors? +
Yes, motors, blades and shutter assemblies can be shipped with a production order so you hold stock locally. Ask for a parts list with your quote.
