Which KLT Size Do I Need?

Start from the room, not the fan. A ventilation fan should change the room air 8–12 times per hour in a bathroom or washroom, and 15–20 times per hour in a kitchen. The table below converts each model's airflow into a practical room size, assuming a 2.5 m ceiling and 10 air changes per hour.

Quick rule of thumb: for a bathroom under 8 m² choose KLT-10B; 12–18 m² choose KLT-12B or KLT-16B; a workshop or canteen up to 30 m² needs KLT-20B; anything above 50 m² or with a duct run over 5 m should move up to KLT-25B or KLT-30B.

Why the auto-shutter matters

Every KLT model carries a gravity auto-shutter behind the grille. When the motor stops, the blades close by their own weight and seal the opening — blocking dust, insects, rain and back-draft from neighbouring rooms. This is the single most requested feature for Middle East and coastal installations, where wind-driven dust and sand would otherwise enter through an open wall fan.
