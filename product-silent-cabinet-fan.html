BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is 18 dB(A) possible? +
The double-skin acoustic housing absorbs motor and airflow noise, and the DC brushless motor runs with almost no mechanical noise. At low speed it is practically inaudible.
Can it run on DC power? +
Yes — 12V/24V DC versions are available for battery or low-voltage systems, common in RVs, yachts and solar setups.
Is it suitable for continuous 24/7 running? +
Yes. The DC brushless motor is designed for continuous duty with a service life of 30,000+ hours.
What about the fresh-air purification unit? +
For whole-room filtration we also offer a HEPA H13 fresh-air purification unit with heat recovery up to 85%. Ask us for details.
