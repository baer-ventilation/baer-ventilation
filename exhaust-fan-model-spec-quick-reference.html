How to Read These Numbers

  • Inch is a mounting size, not airflow. A 6 inch KLT moves 150 m³/h while a 6 inch inline can move far more — compare m³/h, not the inch label. See the sizes guide.
  • Size by air changes per hour (ACH), not guesswork. Bathrooms need 8–12 ACH, kitchens 15–20. Use the CFM calculator or the airflow calculation guide.
  • Noise is dB(A) at the rated point. For sones and dB comparison see the noise levels guide.
  • Certificates cover specific models and voltages. Verify the exact ones for your order on the verification page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exhaust fan models does BAER make?

BAER makes three families: KLT wall/panel exhaust fans in six sizes (6 to 18 inch, 150 to 1,800 m³/h), BXF inline duct fans from 100 to 315 mm, and FA axial fans from FA8-20 to FA16-40. All are factory-direct from our Shengzhou, China facility.

Which KLT model gives the most airflow?

The KLT-30B (18 inch) delivers 1,800 m³/h at 120 W, followed by the KLT-25B (14 inch) at 1,500 m³/h. The smallest, KLT-10B (6 inch), moves 150 m³/h at 30 W. All KLT models are 220 V / 50 Hz as standard.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The standard MOQ is 100 pieces per model, for both stock and OEM/custom orders.

What voltages are available?

KLT panel fans are 220 V / 50 Hz as standard. 110 V / 60 Hz and other voltages are available on OEM orders. For Gulf markets we specify 60 Hz variants; Saudi Arabia is the only 60 Hz Gulf market.

Can I get the net weight for customs declaration?

Packing data is shown per export carton (pieces / CBM / gross weight). Net weight depends on motor winding, cable length and plug type, so it is confirmed on the packing list for each order. Contact us if you need it for a customs declaration.
