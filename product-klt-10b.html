About KLT-10B

KLT-10B is the smallest KLT panel fan — a 6-inch square panel designed for low-noise background ventilation. A 30 W shaded-pole motor moves 150 m³/h of air at just 35 dB, with an integrated auto-shutter that closes when the fan stops to keep out dust, insects and back-draft.

The 200×200 mm front panel fits standard 160×160 mm wall cut-outs, with a Φ85 mm round outlet that connects directly to standard PVC duct or flexible hose. CE-certified for export to the EU, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf states and most of Asia.
