Exhaust Fan Supplier for the UAE

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Ventilation fans for Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah projects — built for high-rise stacks, hospitality standards and 50°C summers.

The UAE ventilation market rewards quiet operation and installation flexibility. High-rise residential towers use centralised ducted extraction with inline fans in ceiling voids; hotels require low dB ratings in guest bathrooms; and coastal humidity means corrosion resistance matters as much as airflow. BAER supplies both the axial units for straightforward wall and window extraction and the mixed-flow inline duct fans that make centralised systems work.

Market Overview

UAE demand is driven by residential towers, hospitality, retail fit-out and warehouse/logistics construction. Buyers typically require quiet operation, cleanable grilles, and documentation accepted by consultants and municipality inspectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your fans suitable for centralised ducted systems in towers?

Yes. Our inline mixed-flow duct fans are designed for duct pressure and can be installed remotely in ceiling voids, keeping occupied spaces quiet — the standard approach for UAE high-rise residential.

What noise levels can you achieve?

Our silent bathroom fan range runs 25–35 dB(A) depending on model. Ask for third-party noise test data for the specific model you are specifying.

Can you do private label for our UAE brand?

Yes — logo printing on the grille or housing, custom packaging, user manuals and barcode service are all available at MOQ from 500 pcs per model.
