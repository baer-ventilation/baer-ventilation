From Resin to Packed Carton: The Production Flow

BAER Ventilation is a vertically integrated manufacturer: housing, motor and assembly all happen under one roof in Shengzhou, Zhejiang. No outsourced housings, no third-party motor supply — which is why our lead times hold even in peak season.

1

Injection Molding

12 in-house injection machines produce fan housings, shutters and grilles from ABS and PP resin — no waiting on external plastic suppliers.

2

Motor Winding

A dedicated winding and stator section builds the motors used across our fan range. Motors are the part that fails first on cheap imports — making our own is the whole point.

3

Assembly

8 assembly lines run wall shutter fans, bathroom and ceiling fans, mixed-flow duct fans and cabinet units as separate flows, so one busy model never starves another.

4

100% Functional Testing

Every fan is powered on and function-tested before packing. Airflow, noise and vibration are sampled at every production stage, not just at the end.

5

Packing & Loading

Export cartons are drop-tested specifications, and container loading is supervised and photographed before the truck leaves.

Built for OEM & ODM Partners

Roughly 20–30% of output is OEM or ODM work: custom voltage and frequency, private-label colour and packaging, and dedicated moulds. In-house injection means we control the tooling instead of renting it from a moulding broker — customisations move faster and stay confidential. Details in the OEM & private label guide.
