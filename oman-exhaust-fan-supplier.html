Certification & Documentation

Oman's market access for electrical goods runs through the Directorate General for Standards and Metrology (DGSM), part of the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Two things apply to a fan shipment, and it is worth keeping them separate.

RequirementWhat it means for your order
G-Mark (GSO)The Gulf-wide conformity mark. Household fans sit explicitly inside the G-Mark scope, based on the GSO adoption of IEC 60335-1 and 60335-2-80, registered through the GSO GCTS platform. This is the primary route for exhaust fans into Oman.
Omani Conformity SchemeEstablished in 2022 under Ministerial Decree 190/2021 and being phased in by product category. Current mandatory type-examination stages cover dishwashers, 10–25 kg washing machines, vacuum cleaners and (from February 2026) water dispensers. Fans are not in a mandatory type-examination stage — but they remain in G-Mark scope, so confirm current status with your importer.
Technical basisGulf Technical Regulation for Low Voltage Electrical Equipment and Appliances BD14200401, covering both safety and EMC.
Oman national differencesTest reports must cover 240V / 50Hz and the Omani plug type. A CB report is the preferred basis, but it has to include those differences — a generic 230V report will not carry through.
Report validityTesting must have been completed within three years of the application date.
Certificate validityOne year, with approved products registered in the DGSM online database.
Local importerThe importer of record must be an Omani-registered company, and Arabic-language product information is required.

The practical route for fans: hold a valid G-Mark built on a CB report that already contains the 240V/50Hz Omani differences, and register the product on the DGSM platform through your Omani importer. Manufacturer or authorised representative may file.

What BAER provides: accredited CB-format test reports covering the Omani national differences, the full technical file, Declaration of Conformity, ISO 9001 evidence and Arabic labelling artwork — everything your Omani importer needs to register the product. Registration is completed by the importer, since the importer of record must be Omani-registered.

Oman's Grid: 240V, 50Hz, Type G

Oman supplies 240V / 50Hz with BS 1363 (Type G) sockets — the same top-of-band voltage as Kuwait and Qatar, and not the 60Hz of Saudi Arabia. Fans for the Sultanate must be nameplated and tested for 240V/50Hz; a report written against generic 230V European conditions will not carry the Omani national difference.

Where Oman differs from its neighbours is in how far the supply has to reach. Distribution runs from dense urban Muscat to isolated industrial sites at Sohar, Duqm and the interior, and voltage drop over long rural feeders is a real consideration. Wide-voltage motors are worth specifying for anything outside the capital region.

Two Climates in One Country: Dhofar's Khareef

Oman is the only Gulf market where a single specification will not cover the whole country, because it has two genuinely different climates.

  • Muscat, Sohar and the north — dry heat with summer ambient of 40–47°C, and coastal salt exposure along the Gulf of Oman. Corrosion-protected finishes and a high insulation class are the specification here.
  • Dhofar and Salalah — the khareef monsoon, roughly June to September, brings persistent high humidity, mist and drizzle to a region that is desert for the rest of the year. Relative humidity stays high for weeks, which is a condensation and corrosion problem rather than a heat problem: specify sealed bearings, corrosion-protected housings and check that the drainage path on any external cowl actually sheds water.
  • The interior — Nizwa and the inland regions run hot and dry, with heavy dust loading. Sealed bearings and washable grilles matter more here than corrosion protection.

A fan specified for Muscat will not necessarily survive a khareef season in Salalah. Tell us the governorate, not just the country.

Frequently Asked Questions

What voltage do you supply for Oman?

240V/50Hz as standard, matching Oman's grid. Other voltages are available on request.

Do you support Gulf conformity documentation?

Yes. We provide the technical file and CE/CB test reports your importer needs to complete Gulf conformity assessment.

What is the MOQ and lead time for Oman orders?

MOQ is 100 pcs per model (mixed models accepted). Lead time is typically 20–30 days after deposit plus 18–25 days sea transit to Sohar or Salalah.

Do exhaust fans need Omani type-examination certification?

Not at present — the mandatory type-examination stages of the Omani Conformity Scheme currently cover dishwashers, 10–25 kg washing machines, vacuum cleaners and water dispensers. Fans instead come in through the Gulf G-Mark, which explicitly covers household fans. Because the scheme is phased in by category, have your importer confirm current scope before each programme.

Why does Salalah need a different specification from Muscat?

Because of the khareef. From roughly June to September, Dhofar sits under monsoon influence with sustained high humidity, mist and drizzle, while Muscat stays dry and hot. That is a condensation and corrosion problem, not a heat one — sealed bearings, corrosion-protected housings and a cowl that actually sheds water are what make the difference over a khareef season.
