About BAER Ventilation

Industrial fan manufacturer with in-house production & global export

BAER Ventilation is a China-based manufacturer and trading company specializing in industrial ventilation equipment. We design, produce and export a full range of exhaust fans, centrifugal fans, axial fans and ventilation accessories for commercial and industrial projects worldwide.

Our Capabilities

  • In-house injection, motor winding and assembly lines
  • OEM/ODM support for brand owners, wholesalers and EPC contractors
  • Certified production with quality control at every stage
  • Export experience to Middle East, Europe and North America

Why Work With Us

  • Factory-direct pricing with stable supply chain
  • Product customization for voltage, airflow and installation type
  • Fast response on quotations and technical selection
  • Full documentation for customs and project bidding

Markets We Serve

Our ventilation fans are used in factories, warehouses, shopping malls, tunnels, farms and residential buildings. Main export markets include Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, Yemen, Europe, Canada and the United States.
