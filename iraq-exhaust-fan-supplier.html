Exhaust Fan Supplier for Iraq

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Ventilation fans built for Iraq's grid conditions and climate — wide-voltage motors, dust-resistant designs, delivery to Umm Qasr and Basra.

Iraq's two defining ventilation requirements are grid stability and price sensitivity. Mains voltage can swing well outside nominal, which destroys standard motors within a season; and the residential market is dominated by simple, affordable window and wall units that are easy to install and replace. BAER supplies wide-voltage motor options (working range roughly 180–250V) and a window-fan range positioned for high-volume residential distribution.

Market Overview

Iraqi demand centres on residential construction and reconstruction in Baghdad, Basra, Erbil and Najaf, plus commercial fit-out and light industrial ventilation. Price-competitive window and wall units dominate volume; hotels and private hospitals specify quieter ceiling and inline models.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can your motors handle voltage fluctuation?

Yes. We offer wide-voltage motor versions with an operating range of approximately 180–250V. This is one of the most requested options for Iraq and is strongly recommended for long motor life.

What is the minimum order for a first trial?

MOQ is 100 pcs per model, and mixed models can be combined into one container. Many Iraqi buyers start with a mix of window and ceiling models to test the market.

Do you provide Arabic packaging?

Yes. Carton artwork, labels and installation manuals in Arabic are available, typically from 500 pcs per model.
