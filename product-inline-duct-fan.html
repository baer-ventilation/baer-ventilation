BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'mixed flow' mean? +
Mixed flow combines axial and centrifugal characteristics: the compact inline form of an axial fan with the pressure capability of a centrifugal fan. That means it can push air through ductwork, filters and bends more effectively.
Which size do I need for my project? +
For a typical bathroom or small room use 100-125mm. For kitchens, apartments or offices use 150-200mm. For long duct runs with multiple bends, choose the high-static-pressure booster models.
Do you offer DC/EC energy-saving versions? +
Yes. Intelligent DC inverter models (125-200mm) use brushless DC motors with PWM / 0-10V control and smart humidity or CO₂ triggers, saving up to 60% energy compared to AC models.
Are the fans certified for EU export? +
Yes — CE certified, with Declaration of Conformity provided with every shipment.
What is the MOQ and lead time? +
Stock models: MOQ 100 pcs, ships in 3-7 days. OEM orders: MOQ 500 pcs, production in 25-35 days.
