Exhaust Fan Supplier for Qatar

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Ventilation fans engineered for Qatar heat and humidity — supplied factory-direct to Doha and Hamad Port.

Qatar runs on a 240V/50Hz grid and combines extreme summer heat with high coastal humidity. A ventilation fan for Doha must handle both — a motor that tolerates high ambient temperatures, and corrosion-resistant finishes for humid, salt-adjacent installations. BAER supplies fans built to these conditions factory-direct, with documentation support for Gulf conformity.

Market Overview

Qatar is a high-value, project-driven market. A decade of infrastructure and hospitality investment — hotels, malls, metro-linked developments and residential compounds — continues under Qatar National Vision 2030. Demand concentrates on bathroom and kitchen extraction in residential and hospitality, plus parking and commercial-kitchen ventilation.

Certification & Documentation

Qatar applies Gulf Standardization Organization (GSO) requirements, with conformity assessment through the Qatar General Organization for Standards and Metrology. BAER provides the technical file, CE/CB test reports and model documentation your importer needs to complete conformity assessment.

Shipping & Logistics

Sea freight from Ningbo/Shanghai to Hamad Port (Doha) typically takes 18–25 days. Air freight is 3–5 days for urgent orders. Terms are FOB Ningbo/Shanghai as standard, with CIF or DDP available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do your fans suit Qatar's hot, humid climate?

Yes. Our Qatar-bound models use high-temperature-rated motors with sealed bearings and corrosion-resistant finishes for humid, coastal installations.

What voltage do you supply for Qatar?

240V/50Hz as standard, matching Qatar's grid. Other voltages are available on request.

What is the MOQ and lead time for Qatar orders?

MOQ is 100 pcs per model (mixed models in one container accepted). Lead time is typically 15–25 days after deposit plus 18–25 days sea transit to Hamad Port.
