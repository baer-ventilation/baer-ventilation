Certification & Documentation

Qatar's import conformity is administered by the Qatar General Organization for Standards and Metrology (QGOSM). Regulated electrical goods — exhaust fans included — must obtain a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) under the Product Conformity Assessment programme before the shipment departs, and the product itself carries the Qatar Conformity Mark (QCM) once certified.

RequirementWhat it means for your order
CoC per shipmentIssued after document review, and where triggered, pre-shipment inspection and testing. The certificate must accompany the shipment — arriving without one risks delay, extra inspection or rejection.
QCM product certificationIssued for the product rather than the shipment, valid three years with annual surveillance. This is what lets repeat shipments clear on documentary checks.
Local representative — mandatoryA non-Qatari entity cannot file directly. A Qatar-resident representative — normally your distributor or a specialist agent — must submit the application and carries continuing responsibility for conformity.
Standards appliedGSO adoptions of the IEC series: GSO IEC 60335-1 and 60335-2-80 for fans, plus GSO CISPR 14-1 and the GSO IEC 61000 series for EMC. Plugs and sockets follow GSO 2530.
CB report conversionAn existing IECEE CB certificate and report can be converted, which shortens the cycle materially — worth confirming before you commission new testing.
Bilingual labellingArabic and English are both required on the product, the packaging and the manual. Qatar applies this strictly, and unchecked machine translation of safety text is a recurring source of market-surveillance disputes.

Practical sequencing: secure the QCM product certification first, then draw a shipment CoC against it for each consignment. Doing it the other way round means repeating testing on every order.

The filing itself is done by your Qatari representative, since only a resident entity can apply. Confirm current scope with them before shipment — the regulated product list is updated periodically.

Shipping & Logistics

Sea freight from Ningbo/Shanghai to Hamad Port (Doha) typically takes 18–25 days. Air freight is 3–5 days for urgent orders. Terms are FOB Ningbo/Shanghai as standard, with CIF or DDP available.

Ports: Hamad Port handles virtually all Qatari container traffic and is the default discharge point; the older Doha port now serves passenger and limited cargo traffic. Terms: FOB Ningbo/Shanghai as standard, with CIF, CFR and DDP available.

Packing: every unit is individually cartoned with foam protection, palletised, and photographed before loading. Air freight to Doha runs 3–5 days and is economic for samples and urgent top-ups below roughly 300 kg.

Sequencing tip: because the shipment CoC is drawn against the QCM product certificate, get the product certification in motion before you book the sailing — certification lead time, not the vessel, is what usually sets the delivery date on a first Qatari order.

Qatar's Grid: 240V, 50Hz, Type G

Qatar supplies 240V / 50Hz with BS 1363 (Type G) three-pin sockets, per GSO 2530. Note the voltage: Qatar sits at the top of the Gulf's 220–240V band alongside Kuwait and Oman, so a fan nameplated only for 220V is a specification mismatch even though it will physically run.

Qatar is not a 60Hz market — that is Saudi Arabia. Getting this wrong costs more than a relabelled nameplate: a 50Hz motor on 60Hz turns roughly 20% faster than designed, drawing more current and shortening motor life. If your order book covers both Doha and Riyadh, specify them as two separate electrical configurations.

Heat, Humidity and Continuous Duty in Qatar

  • Summer heat. Ambient temperatures above 45°C are standard from June to September, and rooftop or façade-adjacent plant rooms run considerably hotter. Check the rated ambient on the datasheet and specify a high insulation class.
  • Gulf humidity and salt. Doha, Lusail and Al Wakrah all sit on a humid, salt-laden coastline. Standard painted steel corrodes fast at that exposure; specify corrosion-protected finishes and stainless fasteners for coastal and podium installations.
  • Continuous operation. In Qatari residential and hospitality projects, bathroom and kitchen extraction commonly runs for long hours rather than intermittently. Size for continuous duty and confirm the bearing specification, not just the airflow figure.
  • Fine dust. Construction activity and shamal winds load grilles quickly. Removable, washable grilles cut the maintenance burden on occupied buildings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do your fans suit Qatar's hot, humid climate?

Yes. Our Qatar-bound models use high-temperature-rated motors with sealed bearings and corrosion-resistant finishes for humid, coastal installations.

What voltage do you supply for Qatar?

240V/50Hz as standard, matching Qatar's grid. Other voltages are available on request.

What is the MOQ and lead time for Qatar orders?

MOQ is 100 pcs per model (mixed models in one container accepted). Lead time is typically 20–30 days after deposit plus 18–25 days sea transit to Hamad Port.

Can a foreign factory apply for Qatari conformity directly?

No. Qatari regulations require a Qatar-resident representative to file and to carry continuing responsibility for conformity — normally your distributor or a specialist agent. We supply the complete technical file and accredited test reports so their submission passes without a query loop.

Should we certify the product or just each shipment?

Certify the product. The QCM product certificate runs three years with annual surveillance, after which each shipment only needs a documentary CoC. Without it you repeat testing on every consignment, which is both slower and more expensive across a project programme.
