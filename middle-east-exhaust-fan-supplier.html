Exhaust Fan Manufacturer for Middle East Markets

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Factory-direct ventilation supply for Saudi Arabia, UAE, Iraq, Kuwait and Qatar — engineered for heat, dust and 50°C+ summers.

The Middle East is one of the most demanding environments for ventilation equipment: ambient temperatures above 45°C, fine airborne sand, high coastal humidity, and — in Saudi Arabia — a 60Hz power grid. BAER Ventilation has supplied distributors, contractors and project buyers across the Gulf since our first shipments to Jeddah, and we build fans specifically for these conditions rather than shipping standard European units into them.

What Gulf Conditions Demand From a Fan

Power supply
220–240V / 50Hz (UAE, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar); 220V / 60Hz (Saudi Arabia — 60Hz motors available across our range)
Climate
Peak summer 45–52°C · fine sand and dust ingress · coastal humidity up to 90% RH · large day/night temperature swings
Motor construction
Sealed or coated windings, high-temperature insulation class, wide-voltage options for unstable grids
Materials
UV-stabilised ABS housings for sun-exposed units, galvanised steel for ceiling and inline units
Filtration
Cleanable grilles and sand-resistant bearings for dusty districts
