BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quiet is this fan really? +
The 100mm model operates at 26 dB(A), which is quieter than a library. Larger sizes are slightly higher but all stay under 30 dB(A) at normal speed.
Can it be used in bathrooms with high humidity? +
Yes for normal residential bathrooms. For direct shower-zone installations we recommend the IP44-rated bathroom series.
What voltage versions are available? +
Standard 220-240V/50Hz for Europe and Middle East; 110V/60Hz for North America is available on request.
Do you offer OEM with our own logo? +
Yes. MOQ 500 pcs for OEM branding, with custom colour and packaging options.
